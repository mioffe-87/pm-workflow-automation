Executive Briefing: CSP Agentic AI StrategyTransforming Support from Reactive Case Handling to Proactive Experience & Self-HealingPresenter: Product ManagementAudience: Sushmita & Executive LeadershipTarget Platform: Palo Alto Networks Customer Support Portal (CSP)1. Executive Framing & VisionOver the past year, our AI strategy has focused on improving the support experience after a customer encounters an issue. We are now exploring how Agentic AI—autonomous agents that detect, reason, and act—can transform the Customer Support Portal (CSP) and broader Palo Alto Networks ecosystems.  While Agentic AI can streamline multiple portal workflows (such as license provisioning or case routing), this proposal focuses on a critical, high-impact case study: detecting and self-healing unseen platform errors before a case is ever opened.2. The Problem & Portal TelemetryEnterprise users frequently encounter unhandled errors (such as Salesforce desyncs, JS 500 exceptions, or SSO deadlocks) and abandon their tasks without creating a ticket.  30-Day Portal Friction Benchmark (Aug 6 – Sep 5, 2026)Total Captured User Events: 27,505,571  Total Unique Visitors: 74,680 across 33,565 Unique Accounts  Friction Signal Volume: 1,931,234 events (~7.02% of all portal interactions)  Dead Clicks: 1,728,664 (~6.28% of total events)  Error Clicks: 95,623 (~0.35%)  Rage Clicks: 79,899 (~0.29%)  U-Turns: 27,048 (~0.10%)  Key Takeaway: Dead clicks occur 18 times more frequently than explicit error clicks. Users are repeatedly clicking non-responsive elements, hitting silent deadlocks, and walking away in frustration without Product or Support ever knowing.  4. Architecture: Signal Engine & 3-Way DispatchThe platform operates as a continuous background listener that processes failures in real time.  Input Layer (Telemetry Capture): Listens to frontend console logs, API status codes (500s), database sync flags, and clickstream distress signals.  Reasoning Layer (Agentic AI Engine): Performs cross-system anomaly detection and automated Root Cause Analysis (RCA) in seconds (e.g., identifying that a role upgrade from Limited to Standard failed to emit the downstream Salesforce Contact Sync).  Multi-Stakeholder Output (3-Way Dispatch):For the User: Displays an in-app status banner setting clear expectations to prevent repeated clicks and frustration.  For Support (CSRs): Auto-generates an enriched engineering ticket in <5s with full diagnostic context.  For Product & R&D: Logs the instance into a daily/weekly Silent Friction Digest surfacing drop-offs that never generated a support ticket.  5. Walkthrough: The "Create a Technical Case" WorkflowScenario: A user is upgraded from Limited to Standard User, but the Salesforce Contact Sync fails in the background. The user clicks "Create a Technical Case".  Current Experience: Cryptic error message or blank screen. The user rage-clicks, gives up, or opens a manual case requiring CSR log tracing.  Phase 1 (Proactive Alert & Auto-Ticket): The AI intercepts the failure instantly. It displays an inline banner informing the user their access is processing, while automatically creating a pre-populated ticket for engineering on their behalf.  Phase 2 (Autonomous Self-Healing): The AI detects the desync, calls the SFDC Admin API directly to force-enable the contact record, and updates local database mapping in <1.4 seconds. The banner turns green ("Sync Complete! Access Granted"), allowing the user to proceed seamlessly without opening a ticket.  6.
