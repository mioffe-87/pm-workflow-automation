Overview

Q: What Palo Alto Networks products are affected by this issue?
This issue is specific to PA-200 devices in the serial number range 001606044723 to 001606075266. We have revisited all existing hardware platforms to ensure the SSDs were specified correctly and have not identified an issue.

Q: What is the failure symptom?
The device may become unresponsive or provide a “merlin” prompt on the console output.

Q: How can I tell if my PA-200 devices are impacted?
Not all PA-200 devices are impacted by this SSD issue; only PA-200 devices in the serial number range 001606044723 to 001606075266 are impacted. Please note, units within this serial number range that were shipped by Palo Alto Networks after November 2016 have already been reworked and equipped with an updated drive and are not impacted by this issue.

Q: The replacement device I received has a serial number that is within the impacted device serial number range. Is it impacted by the SSD issue?
No. All replacement PA-200 devices that have been or will be shipped via RMA or part of this proactive replacement program have been reworked and are equipped with an SSD drive that meets our endurance specifications and are not impacted by this issue.

Q: What is the root cause of this issue?
After conducting an extensive investigation, we have determined that the issue is caused by an SSD drive manufactured in the PA-200 device serial number range 001606044723 to 001606075266. As background, the SSD drive we had been using in the PA-200 was nearing its end-of-life from the manufacturer. The chosen replacement SSD has shown that it has less endurance characteristics than the original drive. As a result, we have seen earlier than expected hardware failures in this specific serial number range of PA-200 devices.

Q: Why is the PA-200 issue being reported now?
It was important to us to do our due diligence and conduct extensive testing to identify the root cause. As soon as we identified the SSD issue in a subset of our PA-200 devices, we immediately began putting a customer care plan in place. This included ensuring we had a full inventory of PA-200 devices to ship out to customers as soon as they requested a replacement device, which took time to build and coordinate with our suppliers. We sincerely apologize to our PA-200 customers who are impacted by the issue and are committed to working with those impacted to remediate any issues.

Q: What is Palo Alto Networks planning to do to prevent this type of issue in the future?
We have conducted a comprehensive assessment of this issue and have actively taken steps to ensure it does not recur in the future, including incorporating additional operational resources to our product design review process to further strengthen our manufacturing quality process controls.

Q: Are there any use cases that contribute to earlier than expected device failures for devices within the impacted serial number range?
The use cases we have observed include instances where there is heavy drive use from logging, packet captures, or frequent dynamic content updates. Unfortunately, there is no way to monitor for earlier than expected product failure.

Remediation Steps

Q: Can I call Global Customer Support to get a replacement device?
For the quickest response, please email PA200-questions@paloaltonetworks.com to request a replacement device and include the serial numbers of your PA-200 devices. If you are experiencing technical issues with your PA-200 device, please open a case online or contact your ASC partner for the quickest response. If you have any questions about the device replacement program, please contact PA200-questions@paloaltonetworks.com and one of our team members will assist you as soon as possible.

Q: What should I do next if my PA-200 device is in the impacted serial number range and I’m not experiencing issues?
If your PA-200 device is in the impacted serial number range 001606044723 to 001606075266, please email PA200-questions@paloaltonetworks.com and we will arrange to have an updated PA-200 device shipped to a location of your choosing. The device will be reworked and equipped with SSD drives that meet our endurance specifications.

Please note, if you have a PA-200 device that is an "on-site spare" (OSS), it will not yet be licensed. For OSS devices, please contact PA200-questions@paloaltonetworks.com to arrange the shipment of any replacement devices.

Q: What should I do if I rely upon partner support or an Authorized Support Center (ASC) for my support services, and/or I don’t have access to manage my devices through the Customer Support Portal?
Should you need immediate assistance or have any questions, please contact your ASC partner. If you aren’t sure who your ASC partner is, or are having trouble reaching them for assistance, please contact us directly at PA200-questions@paloaltonetworks.com and one of our team members will assist you as soon as possible.

Q: I have PA-200 devices in the impacted serial number range that are not yet deployed. What should I do?
If you have any PA-200 devices in the affected serial number range that are not yet deployed, please contact PA200-questions@paloaltonetworks.com and we will work with you to issue proactive replacements for your undeployed PA-200 devices.

Q: When can I expect my proactive replacement?
We will make every effort to fulfill your request as soon as possible and we are committed to ensuring your satisfaction throughout this matter. Because this is a proactive replacement program, it will not follow any existing SLA or Service Contracts.

Q: Where will Palo Alto Networks ship the proactive replacement device(s)?
We will ask you for a preferred mailing address when you request a replacement device at PA200-questions@paloaltonetworks.com.

Q: What should I do after I receive the replacement device?
Please refer to the PA-200 Firewall Migration Guide, which outlines the steps to migrate to your replacement device, including upgrading or downgrading the PAN-OS version to match your original device, migrating the configuration and licenses, and connecting the device to your network. If you have any problems with these steps, please contact the Palo Alto Networks Customer Support team via the normal support channels.

After you have confirmed that the replacement device is fully operational, please return the old unit to us as soon as possible using the included shipping label and box in which we provided the replacement device.

Q: What is the difference between my existing PA-200 and the replacement PA-200?
The replacement PA-200 is equipped with an SSD drive that meets our endurance specifications. In some cases, the replacement PA-200 device that we provide will have a serial number that is within the impacted device serial number range; however, all replacement devices and any PA-200 device provided through an RMA from Palo Alto Networks since November 2016 will already have an updated, reworked SSD drive that meets our endurance specifications.


Customer Support

Q: What units qualify for a replacement?
Units in the serial number range 001606044723 to 001606075266, and are either under an active support contract or still under the 1-year hardware warranty qualify for replacement.

Q: Can I exchange a PA-200 for a PA-220?
No, we will replace the PA-200 with a unit that has the correct SSD drive.

Q: Is Palo Alto Networks providing new or refurbished PA-200 devices for units that they replace?
We are providing a mix of both new and refurbished units. Customers will not be able to specify a preference. The SSD drives for the replacement devices have been quality tested and will be shipped with PAN-OS 7.1.5. Some of the replacement units that we provide via the proactive replacement program will have serial numbers that fall within the impacted device serial number range; however all replacement PA-200 devices provided by this program, regardless of the replacement serial number, will be equipped with an updated SSD drive that meets our endurance specifications.

Q: Is this a product recall?
No, this is not a product recall. Affected PA-200 devices in the specific serial number range are likely to experience a shorter life than expected, although it will vary by usage. Currently, we are working directly with customers who have impacted devices and are offering to proactively replace PA-200 devices within the impacted serial number range.

Q: If I have a PA-200 failure and my device is not in the impacted serial number range, what should I do?
Devices outside of the serial number range 001606044723 to 001606075266 are not impacted by this SSD issue. If your PA-200 device has failed, please contact Palo Alto Networks Customer Support team by opening a case online.

Q: Will the proactive replacement program cover the cost of shipping the devices back to Palo Alto Networks?
Yes, just like our standard RMA process, Palo Alto Networks will ship the replacement PA-200 device(s) with return labels and instructions on how to return it to us.

Q: Does the replacement device have a warranty?
The replacement device will adopt the warranty time that was remaining on the impacted PA-200 device. If the warranty on the impacted device expired by the time we received the replacement device, a warranty period of 30 days will be in effect.

Q: Can you ship my original PA-200 device or SSD drive back to my organization?
No, we cannot ship your original PA-200 device or SSD drive back to you. All drives on impacted devices will be removed and destroyed as part of the proactive replacement process.

Q: Where do I get help for licenses, including support migration, from my existing PA-200 device to the replacement unit?
Please follow the guidelines on PA-200 Firewall Migration Guide. Should any issues occur, please contact Palo Alto Networks Customer Support by opening a case online.

Q: Where do I go if I have more questions not included here?
We are here to help. Should you have any additional questions, please contact PA200-questions@paloaltonetworks.com and one of our support team members will assist you as soon as possible.
