Dear valued Palo Alto Networks customers,

We take the quality of our products very seriously and strive to achieve the highest standards in product quality. We have identified an issue in a subset of our PA-200 devices that will prevent these devices from reaching their full life expectancy and can result in a hardware failure earlier than expected.

We have outlined below the root cause of this issue, the steps we are taking and have already undertaken to remediate it, and how to engage with us for any questions you may have or assistance you need to remediate any related issues.

We are committed to ensuring your satisfaction throughout this matter. We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience and any issues this may cause.

ROOT CAUSE

After conducting an extensive investigation, we have determined that the issue is caused by an SSD drive manufactured in the PA-200 device serial number range 001606044723 to 001606075266 that does not have the required endurance specifications. This drive was a replacement drive for one that was end-of-life. Due to the endurance limitations, we have seen earlier than expected hardware failures in this specific serial number range of PA-200 devices.

We take full responsibility for this error and have actively taken steps to ensure this issue does not recur in the future, including incorporating additional operational resources to our product design review process to further strengthen our manufacturing quality process controls.

REMEDIATION STEPS

For PA-200 devices within the serial number range above that are impacted by this SSD issue, we have a device replacement program in place. Please see the following:

  • If your organization’s PA-200 devices are within the impacted serial number range, we are offering to provide replacement devices that have been reworked and are equipped with SSD drives that meet our endurance specifications. Registered spare units are also eligible for this program.
  • To receive a replacement device, please email PA200-questions@paloaltonetworks.com and we will arrange to have an updated PA-200 device shipped to a location of your choosing. Electing to receive a replacement device does not modify your existing support contract or subscriptions.
  • Please note, you must have an active support contract that covers PA-200 devices within the serial number range in order to participate in this program. If you choose not to replace your PA-200 device at this time, you will still be eligible to do so as long as the device is under an active support contract.
